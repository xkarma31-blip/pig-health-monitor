3. CORRECTED FACTS — THESE SUPERSEDE ALL OLDER ENTRIES
=====================================================================
A. THERMAL GEOMETRY
   MLX90640 has two FOV variants: BAA = 110x75 deg, BAB = 55x35 deg.
   This project uses the 110 deg BAA part.
   Ground footprint at 2 m: 17.9 x 12.8 cm per pixel (110 deg part).
   The figure "6 x 5 cm per pixel at 2 m" belongs to the 55 deg BAB part and
   must never be quoted for this build.
   A pig ear ROI is about 0.22 px at 2 m with the 110 deg part, and about
   1.46 px with the 55 deg part. SUB-PIXEL IN BOTH CASES.
   Therefore: ear-temperature measurement and individual pig identification
   are IMPOSSIBLE at pen distance with this sensor. This is a geometry
   result, not a tuning problem. Do not describe it as fixable in software.
   A "570 px per pig" figure was withdrawn — it did not reproduce.

B. EMISSIVITY CORRECTION
   The relation is multiplicative in ABSOLUTE temperature: T_true ~ T / e^0.25
   It MUST be applied in KELVIN, never Celsius.
   Correct magnitude: about +1.74 C for a 0.978 emissivity. Applying it in
   Celsius gives about +0.21 C — wrong by a factor of about 8.
   That error is LARGER than the 1.5 C fever threshold, so it is
   defence-critical. Also check for DOUBLE correction: the MLX90640
   CalculateTo() routine already accounts for emissivity.
   Emissivity-on-skin reference: 0.978.

C. POWER
   "About 30 mA average" and "about 170 hours" cannot both be true.
   9.41 mA -> about 170 h.  30 mA -> about 53.3 h.
   Cite one or the other with its own assumption; never both.

D. SPECTRAL SUBTRACTION
   The claim "spectral subtraction hurts deep learning" is CONTRADICTED.
   SS-ESC 2024 found it improved classification performance, and a 2026
   pig-cough localisation paper (adaptive SS + FIR + KL-NMF) uses it
   successfully. The design choice stands on other grounds (noise-injection
   augmentation is simpler to validate) — not on that false claim.

E. HEALTH-SCORE MAPPING
   The documented input/output pairs are NOT linear. Any interpolation must
   state its transfer function explicitly. Do not silently assume linearity.

F. CITATIONS
   The "99.7%" figure belongs to the Edge Impulse project "When Pigs AI" —
   NOT to "Oduor et al." Do not attribute it to that paper.
   UNTRACEABLE (do not cite until located): PHP 6B loss, Frontiers 2023,
   JMBM 2025, Baluyut 2019, Jia et al.
   If you cannot locate a source, write "untraceable" — never cite a paper
   you cannot open.
