Here are the prioritized visible defects of the prototype render compared to the approved design:

### 1. Label & Indicator Defects
* **Illegible Subtext:** The tagline under "PigPulse" is extremely dark and virtually invisible against the black background.
* **Missing Status LED:** The green operational LED below the main logo is completely absent.

### 2. Housing Shape & Geometry
* **Flat Top Module:** The top thermal housing is a simple flat box, lacking the required downward-angled face/hood for pen coverage.
* **Overly Blocky / Sharp Edges:** Enclosures use raw 90° sharp corners instead of the rounded, filleted edges of the ruggedized IP65 enclosure.
* **Missing Enclosure Hardware:** Entirely lacks the side latches, hinges, external mounting tabs, and molded structural seams.

### 3. Ports & Connectors
* **Missing Module Interconnects:** The locking waterproof circular connectors between the main body and the top/bottom modules are missing, leaving the modules floating detached.
* **Simplified Apertures:** The thermal and acoustic openings are reduced to simple flat green rings rather than recessed lenses, gaskets, and acoustic grilles.
