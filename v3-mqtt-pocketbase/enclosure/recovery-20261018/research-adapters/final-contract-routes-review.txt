### P2 — Render-only geometry modifiers can pass the export contract

**File:** `check_integrated_export_contract.py:100–107, 244–247`

`geometry_modifiers()` checks the **viewport dependency graph**, not render evaluation. A geometry-changing modifier with `show_viewport=False` and `show_render=True` therefore produces an unchanged evaluated mesh and returns `[]`.

For a nonthermal manufacturing instance such as `main_lid`, this leaves the other acceptance checks inspecting its unchanged base mesh: `volume()`, `bounds()`, `triangle_hash()`, and `vertex_distance()` all read `obj.data`. The Boolean probe likewise does not establish equality with the render-evaluated geometry. Consequently, the contract can accept a scene whose render geometry differs from the mapped manufacturing STL.

**Concrete negative case:** add a render-enabled, viewport-disabled Solidify modifier to `main_lid`, without changing its base mesh or transform.
