## Findings — severity order

### 1. High — All four tie tunnels terminate inside the bearing legs
**`build_mic_corner_support.py:33–41`**

The tunnel cutter does not reach the inward face of the adjacent corner leg:

- Positive-X leg occupies **X = 5.5…7 mm**.
- Its stop occupies **X = 7…8.5 mm**.
- The 4 mm tunnel cutter centered at X = 7.75 reaches only **X = 5.75…9.75 mm**.

That leaves a **0.25 mm solid wall at X = 5.5…5.75**, across the tunnel’s Y/Z opening. The negative-X side has the mirrored defect. Consequently, these are outward-opening **blind pockets**, not through-tunnels: a tie inserted from outside cannot emerge underneath the PCB and continue around it. Prethreading does not solve the closed end.

**Practical completion:** extend each cut beyond the inward leg face, then check that a continuous reserved tie-width/thickness envelope actually connects the openings to a route over the board. This can be completed using a declared purchased-tie envelope; actual hardware measurements are not necessary just to reserve that space.

### 2. Medium — Installed checker can pass despite unusable retention and weakened/disconnected seat geometry
**`check_mic_corner_installed.py:38–49`**

The acceptance condition checks four center-point seat rays and solid intersections only. It does **not** test:

- Through-connectivity of the tie tunnels—the defect above passes these checks.
- A continuous tie route, including bends and a reserved head location.
- Finite bearing area under the PCB.
- A connected load path from each bearing seat into the base.
- Manifoldness of the imported support.

A ray hitting a tiny remaining seat, or even a detached seat island, can satisfy the present bearing test. Non-intersection is not evidence of retention.

**Practical completion:** add explicit geometric acceptance checks for tunnel exit/connectivity, reserved tie-route clearance, finite corner contact patches, and seat-to-base connectivity. Keep acoustic/component validation separate rather than making it a prerequisite for this nominal-envelope completion.
