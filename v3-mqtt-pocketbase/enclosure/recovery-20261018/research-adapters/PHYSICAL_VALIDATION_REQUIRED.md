# Physical validation — not performed

This deliverable is a research-dimensioned digital prototype. No printed
enclosure, board fit, clamping torque, retention load, environmental seal,
acoustic transfer or thermal measurement was physically tested in this work.
Published dimensions and engineering scenarios are not measured board facts.

Before assembly/use:
- Identify exact PCB/connector variants and measure outline, stack and port/
  pupil positions. Inspect every proposed edge-contact and lacing lane for
  underside components and exposed conductors. Reject unsafe lanes.
- Print clearance coupons for M3/M2 seats, access holes, mic tunnels and lacing
  cord. Inspect support removal, layer adhesion and dimensional variation.
- Dry-fit boards without force. Check insulation, component clearance, lid
  margin, wire bend/connector insertion, strain relief and assembly sequence.
- Use declared low-profile thermal fasteners/nuts, not arbitrary replacements;
  verify thread engagement, compression feet and retention without board flex.
- Verify lacing cord/knot security, abrasion and tension. Conventional cable
  ties tested in the larger scenario do not fit these microphone tunnels.
- Test mounting/strap loads, vibration and impact with realistic print material
  and orientation before attachment in a barn. No rated load is supplied.
- Measure thermal field/temperature bias and microphone response against a
  baseline. The optical ray grid is conditional and cannot establish actual
  pupil alignment, calibration or an unvignetted continuous field.
- Perform dust/moisture/cleaning and electrical safety tests before livestock
  deployment. No IP rating, weather resistance or animal-safe deployment claim.

External executable dependencies are not included. A reproducible digital pass
does not turn any of the above into a completed physical test.
