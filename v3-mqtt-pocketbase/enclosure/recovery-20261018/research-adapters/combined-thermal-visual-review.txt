### 1. Concrete Visible Defects (Geometry, Fit & Mesh)

1. **Optical Trim / Window Cutout Intersection on Thermal Module:**
   - The white/light adapter frame (thermal aperture adapter) visually clips through the rectangular window opening of the top module faceplate.
   - The top flange and bottom bar of the adapter frame sit flush or slightly protrude beyond the exterior face bevel without an intentional bezel recess or clear perimeter margin.

2. **Acoustic Face Trim Ring Orientation & Recess:**
   - In the candidate, the dual green circular trims protrude forward past the module face plane with deep center bore voids.
   - On the original poster, the dual circular microphone ports sit fully recessed beneath outer dark chamfered apertures with flush inner mesh grills.

3. **Fastener Hole Depth & Counterbore Geometry:**
   - The 4 corner fastener points on the main lid and modules appear as flat cylindrical blind indentations/stamps rather than deep counterbored pockets or through-holes suitable for standard socket-head fasteners.

---

### 2. Required Mechanical Adaptations (Intentional / Functional Changes)

1. **SHT Sensor Mount Extension:**
   - **Observed:** An outboard horizontal arm carrying a single primary base plate and an outboard support frame extending from the right upper flank.
   - **Context:** In accordance with specifications, this is an intentional single base plus outboard support frame (not a duplicate part), required to position the environmental sensor outside the enclosed thermal/acoustic envelope for ambient air exposure.

2. **Thermal Module Internal Fastening / Adapter Plate:**
   - **Observed:** The white internal bracket/adapter frame installed directly behind the thermal face cutout to secure the MLX90640 sensor.
   - **Context:** Internal rear inserts/mounting provisions adapted to mechanically capture the discrete sensor breakout board behind the front aperture.

3. **Modular Inter-Module Spacing & Standoffs:**
   - Modules sit separated along the vertical axis with dedicated clearance gaps to accommodate wiring pass-through and modular assembly fixtures.

---

### 3. Source Aesthetic Deviations (Stylistic / Rendering Differences)

1. **Surface Finish & Material Shading:**
   - **Poster:** Matte textured industrial plastic with subtle edge highlights, molded parting lines, and specularity.
   - **Candidate:** Uniform flat charcoal/dark slate clay shading with minimal texture mapping or edge wear.

2. **Branding & Graphic Typography:**
   - **Poster:** "PigPulse" logo with thin subtitle ("Edge Intelligence for Swine Health") and a distinct green LED indicator lens below.
   - **Candidate:** Graphic imprint is larger and bolder relative to lid face dimensions; LED dot is rendered as a simple green sphere rather than a flush translucent light pipe.

3. **Module Proportions & Chamfers:**
   - Corner fillets on the main body and top module in the candidate have a slightly sharper radius than the softer, molded-tooling draft angles seen on the poster’s exploded and internal view renders.
