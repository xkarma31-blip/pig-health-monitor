# Thermal bridge: do not install as universal adapter

The42×12mm opening supplies bearing to small published PCB examples, but narrows
the optical passage. Conditional centered planar ray budget with chosen1mm edge
allowance fails wide110×75° example at8mm setback and greater. Narrow55×35°
example fails the vertical budget at16mm. These are analytical hypotheses, not
measured optical tests; tilted/thick adapter and misalignment can be worse.

Therefore this planar coupon is NOT promoted to P or approved for either actual
sensor. A sensible final architecture must support PCB underside/edges behind
the pupil without letting support rails enter the expanding forward optical cone.
Overall module height10.5/16mm does not establish pupil setback. Retain generous
existing hood aperture and use a board-specific rear/edge support or positively
located adjustable seat. Do not enlarge case blindly to fix a support-path issue.

Research has established room requirements and exposed a genuine unsupported
board assumption. It has not established a universally safe rail placement. The
next digital experiment should use an explicit PCB-plane/pupil-offset scenario,
supporting the rear PCB while projecting the sensor forward through the aperture.
