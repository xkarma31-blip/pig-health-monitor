## Findings, in severity order

### 1. Medium — A 4 mm lid ear conflicts with the specified 3 mm lid-side envelope

**References: lines 91–96, 125–127.**

The proposed tub ears occupy **z=26…30**. With the lid translated upward by 30 mm, its specified local **z=0…3** becomes **assembly z=30…33**. These meet correctly at z=30.

However, **12×14×4 cannot describe both halves while retaining those envelopes**:

- Tub half: 4 mm thick is consistent.
- Lid half: use **3 mm thickness**, or explicitly accept a locally thicker upper boss.
- Do not center a 4 mm lid ear on local z=1.5: it would occupy −0.5…3.5 and intersect the tub ear in assembly.

**Recommendation:** tub ears **12×14×4**, lid ears **12×14×3**, with mating faces at assembly z=30. If 4 mm upper ears are wanted, put them at local z=0…4, accepting 1 mm proud of the lid—not below its mating face.

### 2. Medium — “4 mm overlap” is not 4 mm of wall engagement

**References: lines 79–83, 108.**

For a 12 mm X-width ear centered at **x=−62**, its bounds are **x=−68…−56**. The straight left wall occupies **x=−60…−57**.

Thus:

- Overlap inside the tub’s outside outline: **4 mm**.
- Actual wall intersection: **3 mm**, the full nominal wall thickness.
- Extension into the cavity: **1 mm**.

This is a genuine positive-volume attachment, unlike an ear that only touches the outer wall. But rounding the ear’s inner corners and applying edge relief reduces the attachment near the ends; the full 14 mm Y-span should not be treated as an uninterrupted rectangular web.

**Recommendation:** retain a **broad, solid root across the wall**, with modest plan corner radii, approximately **2 mm** as a concept starting point. Keep the bolt-bearing surfaces flat. Do not hollow or neck down the root merely to resemble a boss. This establishes a credible connection geometrically; it does **not** establish load capacity.

### 3. Low — Additional fasteners provide local clamping, but also add removal steps

**References: lines 84–95.**

Existing corner axes are **(±54, ±50)**. The proposed side axes **(−64, ±32)** are approximately **20.6 mm** from their nearest corner axes. They are therefore distinct closure locations rather than duplicate holes at the corner posts.

Their meaningful contribution is local left-edge restraint. They do not create a hinge: **all applicable corner and supplementary fasteners must be released before straight lid removal**.

A clearance bore alone is not a closure. Specify purchased **through bolts plus nuts**, with heads and nuts bearing on the outer faces of the paired ears. Do not imply that an unthreaded lower bore retains the screw.
