### Visual Comparison: Approved Poster vs. Current 3D Enclosure Render

---

### 1. Aesthetic Correspondence & Core Features

* **Charcoal Three-Module Silhouette**: 
  * **Retained.** The stacked three-tiered layout (compact top module, large central body, horizontal bottom module) is preserved.
  * The dark charcoal/matte slate-grey body finish remains consistent across all sections.

* **Dual Mint Ports**:
  * **Retained.** The lower module features two circular apertures highlighted with prominent mint-green accent rings/bezels.

---

### 2. Concrete Discrepancies & Observed Mismatches

#### **A. Inter-Module Interconnects & Spacing**
* **Approved Poster**: The top and bottom modules connect directly to the main body via ruggedized, visible circular locking collars/quick-disconnect connectors centered between the modules.
* **Current Render**: The three modules appear to "float" with no visible physical conduit, cables, or locking connectors bridging the top or bottom gaps.

#### **B. Top Module Aperture & Beveling**
* **Approved Poster**: Houses a forward/downward-canted, compact circular lens fixture set inside a rounded, hooded rectangular shroud with external corner fasteners.
* **Current Render**: Features a wide rectangular cutout/recess with a sharp internal wedge/slant, lacking the distinct rounded hooded housing and centered lens bezel.

#### **C. Acoustic / Bottom Module Detailing**
* **Approved Poster**: 
  * The two circular microphone ports have dark, perforated mesh/grilles flush with dark surrounds, while the mint accents in the design system represent connectors/status rather than thick outer gaskets on the front face.
  * Shows bottom-facing/angled port geometry indicated in callouts.
* **Current Render**: 
  * Displays two thick, flat mint-green ring bezels on the front face with dark voids inside (no visible acoustic grille texture).
  * Includes four visible countersunk screw points arranged in pairs around each port, which are absent on the front face of the poster's acoustic pod.

#### **D. Mounting Fixture & Enclosure Hardware**
* **Approved Poster**: Features integrated side flanges/lugs with dual-bolt clamp points on the left and right sides of the main body (designed for pole, post, or wall brackets).
* **Current Render**: Has smooth, flat sides on the main enclosure (no lateral mounting ears/hinge lugs), and instead adds an articulated wall-mount bracket arm protruding off the rear-right side of the top/main junction.

#### **E. Surface Fasteners & Chamfers**
* **Approved Poster**: Has robust exterior corner screw bosses on the front perimeter and distinct molded seam lines/radii.
* **Current Render**: Uses flat countersunk corner screw recesses directly on the front panel face, with simpler, sharper box geometry and minimal edge rounding compared to the molded look in the poster.
