# Unfinished integration geometry — not merely pending tests

These interfaces are absent or provisional. Do not treat an exported carrier as
a completed sensor installation. Measurements below are required to finish CAD;
manufacturer package dimensions alone do not identify a fitted breakout.

## Microphone sound path

Deliverable: removable breakout-specific seat and local acoustic gasket seat,
with port aligned to the face aperture and no broad unintended bypass cavity.
Needed: actual board identity, bottom/top-port side, port center relative to PCB
edges, PCB thickness, component/solder protrusions, board-to-face distance and
compressible gasket material. Keep the O strap escape repair. Acoustic response
still requires enclosure-on/off measurements after geometry is completed.

## Thermal installation

Deliverable: board-specific cradle retention and fitted aperture relationship,
with documented lens/pupil coordinates and free zone. Needed: actual breakout,
lens variant, pupil-to-board/face distance, connector and strap keep-outs. Existing
N-derived ray sensitivity sweeps show risks, not an approved seat position.
Do not install a decorative restrictive optic or ordinary glass to fill the view.

## Complete strap route

Deliverable: measured tie bend/head route around each installed board, without
pinching between carrier and face. Needed: selected tie width, thickness, head
dimensions, permissible bend and board keep-outs. Existing straight corridor and
selected floor/annulus probes are narrower evidence than a full installed route.

## Cable connection guards

Deliverable under digital investigation: removable guard between each pod and
main case, preserving existing through-bolted spine and service access. Final
routing requires wire/bundle diameter, insulation, connectors, bend radius and
strain-relief selection. A guard is not a cable gland, structural coupling or seal.
Current upper exits differ in depth; a straight coaxial connector is not modeled.

## SHT installation

Deliverable: actual GY-SHT board retention with sensor exposed, thermal separation
and component-safe tie route. Needed: resolve SHT31/SHT45 identity, PCB dimensions,
sensor location, connector and tie keep-outs. Open carrier is not dust/water or
chemical protection. Air exposure does not establish representative sampling.
