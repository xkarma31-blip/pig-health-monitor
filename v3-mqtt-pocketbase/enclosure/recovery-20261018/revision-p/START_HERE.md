# P — reviewed digital prototype with supplementary side closure

Latest coherent digital deliverable; preserved O remains available for comparison.
P retains O microphone relief and adds two genuine split through-bolted ears on
the main tub/lid left edge. These supply supplementary local edge clamping;
they are not hinges, wall mounts, twistlocks or proof of sealing.

Design basis: focused image review reports paired side bosses, but their source
function is unestablished. This is a functional interpretation, not an exact
mechanism replica. Existing four corner bolts remain required. Remove all six
main-cover fasteners before straight lid removal.

Ear plan bounds X=-70..-56, station Y=±32, bore X=-65.5, nominal diameter3.4mm.
Tub halves Z=26..30, lid halves local Z=0..3; installed lid translation30mm.
The experiment found all-edge bevel removed flat nut support. P therefore rounds
only vertical ear edges and adds ears after global edge relief, preserving the
outer bearing planes. Hardware dimensions remain assumptions, not measured fit.

Installed-service follow-up found nominal10mm-diameter rear nut tool collided
with the wall at the former X=-64 axes. Those failing exports and report are
preserved in pre-tool-access-repair/. Axes moved to X=-65.5 and ears widened
outward2mm, providing nominal0.5mm wall/tool radial separation. This is still an
assumed cylindrical tool, not a measured socket/hand-access approval.

P has its own verification and packaging runners. No inherited O success claim
substitutes for P-specific checks. See current status below and retained logs.
