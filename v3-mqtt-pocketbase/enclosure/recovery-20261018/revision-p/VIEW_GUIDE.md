# Read the assembly views together

front-and-rear-assembly.png pairs the presentation render with the rear view.
The front view alone conceals the common bolted rear spine; pods are not floating.
Actual STL-based assembly and20-instance collision inventory document attachment.

The right-offset open carrier is for GY-SHT, not a wall-mount bracket. Its rigid
arm bolts to the spine; it does not join the main lid. Side ears exist on the left
only. They are supplemental bolted closure, not symmetrical hidden hinges.

Thermal opening is intentionally empty: no installed lens, window or fabricated
sensor surrogate. Actual fitted optics require measured board/pupil geometry.
Mint acoustic rings are optional trim, not mesh, membrane or acoustic validation.
Raster artwork and purchased fastener/LED proxies are render-only, not STLs.

Final image reviewer supports readable wordmark/tagline and visible ear screws.

view concealing the spine. It also calls hex recesses cross/slotted; script uses
six-sided cutters. These incorrect inferences are preserved in its full report,
not promoted into CAD requirements or factual descriptions.

Connector appearance remains uncertain across contradictory reviews. No dummy
locking couplings, restrictive lens, gasket or hinge was invented to fill a view.
