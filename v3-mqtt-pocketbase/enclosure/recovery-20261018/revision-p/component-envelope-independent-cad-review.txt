### 1. High — microphone 22 × 20 × 12 reservation conflicts with carrier hardware

Each carrier is **26 × 26 × 3**, with bolt axes at **(±10, ±10)** and a central **Ø12** sound opening. A centered 22 × 20 footprint occupies x = ±11, y = ±10: **all four bolt axes fall within or on its footprint**. Rotating the box 90° does not resolve that.

The apparent 26 × 26 platform is therefore not an unobstructed board envelope. With a conservative **3.5 mm radial hardware keep-out**, the straight corridor between opposite fastener rows is only **13 mm**, not 20–22 mm.

- The carrier’s board-facing surface is at acoustic depth **27 mm**; the tub floor ends at depth **3 mm**. This gives **24 mm gross rearward depth**, before rear-mount hardware and wiring.
- A 12 mm-deep box extending rearward from that surface occupies approximately depth **15–27 mm**: depth is relatively generous, but the fastening plane is obstructed.
- Ledger M3×12 bolts through the **6 mm face/carrier stack** leave **6 mm beyond the stack**, if installed with their threaded ends toward the board. Reversing them still leaves head/washer keep-outs.

**Alternative to investigate:** keep the microphone centered on its acoustic port, but elevate its reservation beyond the installed fastener envelopes using a purpose-designed support. Approximately **7 mm elevation plus 12 mm board depth** fits inside the gross 24 mm rearward space, leaving about **5 mm**, before cables and actual hardware. That is a packaging direction, not approval of the existing carrier.
