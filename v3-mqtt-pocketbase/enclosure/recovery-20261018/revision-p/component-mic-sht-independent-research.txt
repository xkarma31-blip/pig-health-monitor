### 3. CAD Envelopes & Engineering Allowances

#### A. Conservative CAD Envelopes (Worst-Case Physical Boundary)
When sizing enclosures, retaining clips, or interior clearance volumes without knowing the exact supplier lot:

* **INMP441 Breakout:**
  * **Envelope Box (Rectangular):** **16.5 mm (L) × 13.5 mm (W) × 13.5 mm (H)** (assuming vertical straight header pins installed, bottom solder tails untrimmed).
  * **Envelope Cylinder (Round):** **Ø15.5 mm × 13.5 mm (H)**.
  * *Acoustic clearance note:* The INMP441 is a bottom-port microphone. The breakout PCB has a sound port hole (typically Ø0.8 mm to Ø1.2 mm). An unblocked path or gasket sealing zone of at least Ø2.5 mm around the PCB port must be preserved in the CAD envelope.

* **GY-SHT31 / GY-SHT45 Breakout:**
  * **Envelope Box (Full 6-pin GY module):** **20.0 mm (L) × 17.0 mm (W) × 13.5 mm (H)** (with straight headers installed) or **6.0 mm (H)** if using a right-angle header or direct-soldered wire harness.
  * **Mounting Hole Centers:** Center-to-center spacing is nominally 15.0 mm for boards with two 2.0 mm screw holes.
  * *Environmental clearance note:* SHT sensing windows require passive convective exposure; do not seal the top face flush against a solid enclosure wall.

---

#### B. Separately Defined Arbitrary Engineering Allowances
Arbitrary safety margins recommended for 3D printing (FDM/SLA) and assembly variations:

1. **PCB Edge Routing / V-Cut Tolerance:** **+0.3 mm to +0.5 mm** on board length and width to absorb non-deburred breakout tabs or panelization mouse bites.
2. **Component & Solder Joint Clearance:** **+0.8 mm to +1.2 mm** on the board face for hand-soldered joints, wire solder balls, or bulky flux residue.
3. **FDM 3D Print Shrinkage & Wall Fit:** **+0.35 mm radial clearance** (0.7 mm diametrical) for internal pocket walls to prevent binding due to printer calibration variance.
4. **Wiring & Connector Bend Radius Allowance:** **+15.0 mm to +20.0 mm** along the header axis if using pre-crimped DuPont jumper wires, or **+6.0 mm** if direct-soldering stranded wires with heat-shrink strain relief.
